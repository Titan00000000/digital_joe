About Joe | 20+ Years in Computer Science & AI Leadership
Hi, I’m Timur Rahman but most people know me as Joe
20+ years of teaching, coaching, and solving data challenges.
Patience
First
With decades in education, there are no "silly questions."
Practical
Focus
I focus on real business results (saving time, increasing clarity), not flashy technical hype.
End-to-End
Experience
From creating training content to building data and AI solutions, Digital Joe handles the technical heavy lifting so clients don't have to.
A little bit about me...
The Origin of Joe
Officially, I’m Timur Rahman, but I’ve been known as Joe (from my middle name, Yusuf) since primary school. When I launched my consultancy, Digital Joe was the natural choice.
From 48k of RAM to the Future of AI
My passion for tech started at age eight, coding a simple pixel-dodging game on a ZX Spectrum 48k after being inspired by the movie
WarGames
. That early spark grew into an A-Level, BSc, and MSc in Computer Science, along with a lifelong love for technology and gaming.
20+ Years of Real-World Experience
I spent over two decades teaching Computer Science. When I took over my school’s struggling department, we turned it around to rank in the top 20% nationally. That experience—alongside serving as a governor at Bury College—taught me how to communicate complex ideas clearly and implement digital tools strategically across organisations.
Demystifying Data Science & AI
Since 2021, I’ve coached professionals in Data Science and AI, stripping away the jargon to make learning interactive, practical, and hands-on. I help clients navigate AI strategy, generative and agentic AI, and data engineering so they can turn raw data into automated insights.
Ready to Get Started?
Explore how we can work together, or head over to the contact page to drop me a message!
